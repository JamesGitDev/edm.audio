Uplink Listener beta 0.3.16-rc3
November 2, 2026

Install as an update; keep your existing Uplink app and sign-in.

Song covers now restore a matching saved thumbnail on the first player frame, then improve its resolution. Missing or unavailable covers use a neutral dark area. Artwork updates preserve playback; older image requests cannot replace a newer song or another account's artwork.

Persistent sign-in, optional biometric sign-in and passkeys, live radio, personal listening, and your library remain available. Android requires your approval for credential-provider setup where needed.

This is a public beta. Full broadcast-change/reopen, optional app-lock unlocking, alternate-appearance cold starts, and locked-screen presentation still need wider validation. Core artwork behavior was tested on a Pixel 10 Pro XL and an Android emulator.

Automatic phone-to-phone sign-in transfer remains disabled. Use your passkey, account password or email recovery on a replacement phone.
