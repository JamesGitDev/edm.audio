Uplink Listener beta 0.3.16-rc5
November 3, 2026

Install as an update to your existing Uplink app; do not uninstall it first.

After your initial account sign-in, Uplink offers Android confirmation for quick
sign-in automatically. You do not need to enable it in Uplink Settings. Once
confirmed, the welcome screen makes biometrics or device unlock the main option.
Your phone's supported fingerprint, face or screen-lock method is used.

Uplink continues keeping your normal session signed in. Canceling optional
quick-sign-in setup does not sign you out or interrupt your music. Passkeys,
email/password and email recovery remain available as supported alternatives.

Improved Library controls for small screens and larger text. Liked Songs,
Saved Songs, search and sorting remain usable while song lists can scroll.
The prior artwork, player-control and persistent-session improvements remain.

This is a beta release tested with Android 16/API36 emulator PIN authentication.
Physical phone/fingerprint testing continues. Phone-to-phone automatic account
restoration remains disabled pending two-device validation.
