Uplink Listener beta 0.3.16-rc4
November 3, 2026

Install as an update; keep your existing Uplink app and sign-in.

Removed the extra Undo feedback / skip arrow from the player controls. Tap Like again to remove your like; the existing count, saving and playback behavior are preserved.

The rc3 artwork improvements, persistent sign-in, optional biometric sign-in and passkeys, live radio, personal listening and your library remain available.

This is a public beta. The rc4 build, existing unit tests, signing and release checks were run. The extra rc4 emulator visual check was unavailable because automatic tool approval timed out. Wider device, optional app-lock, alternate-appearance cold-start, broadcast-change/reopen and locked-screen testing continues.

Automatic phone-to-phone sign-in transfer remains disabled. Use a passkey, password or email recovery on a replacement phone.
